    How to read it

    Architecture carried by a compact structural core

    The model separates the floating white galleries from the steelwork that gathers their loads into rings, a diagrid and ten columns. The oculus turns the roof into a water collector as well as a sculptural surface.

    Gallery fingersTen differently proportioned upper halls are represented as stepped, curving brick volumes around the atrium.
    Primary structureRadial and finger trusses meet ring members and a lattice diagrid before loads reach the principal columns.
    Lower galleriesA second exhibition zone sits below the water-lily garden; use the section cut to reveal it.
    Water storyRun the rain to see roof water converge on the central oculus and drop through the open-air atrium.
